# ADR-006: Distributed Governance Foundation

**Status:** Proposed\
**Target Phase:** Phase 3

## Context

HireRise is evolving into a modular platform with multiple services,
workers, and governance domains.

## Decision

Introduce a Distributed Governance Foundation to centralize policy
enforcement, audit events, approval workflows, and governance metadata.

## Rationale

-   Consistent governance across services
-   Centralized auditability
-   Scalable operational controls
-   Support future distributed components

## Consequences

Positive: - Improved compliance and traceability - Consistent governance
model

Trade-offs: - Additional operational complexity - New governance
services to maintain
