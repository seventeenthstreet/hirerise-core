# ADR-007: Intelligence Foundation Layer

**Status:** Proposed\
**Target Phase:** Phase 3

## Context

AI capabilities are expanding beyond resume analysis into career
intelligence, recommendations, analytics, and forecasting.

## Decision

Create a shared Intelligence Foundation Layer providing reusable AI
services, embeddings, scoring, recommendations, and orchestration.

## Rationale

-   Avoid duplicated AI logic
-   Standardize model interactions
-   Simplify future AI feature development

## Consequences

-   Shared AI platform services
-   Easier integration of future models
-   Centralized monitoring and governance
